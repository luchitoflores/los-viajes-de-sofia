## Terreno: prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Production 2D game asset, NOT concept art: one bare perfectly circular tiny planet terrain disk, orthographic front cross section, centered, genuine transparent background, square 1536px. Storybook watercolor and fine painterly gouache, beautifully illustrated professional children's adventure, warm honey limestone, terracotta earth with tiny stratified rock details and moss pockets inside, a thin continuous grassy olive-green rim ALL AROUND the entire smooth circular circumference. The walkable edge must be a mathematically round disk, exact center at image center, radius 44% of image width; no protrusions beyond rim, no background halo or cast shadow outside disk. No buildings, no characters, no trees, no objects, no baked in props: all gameplay objects will be separate sprites. Rich hand-painted geological texture, restrained soft warm lighting without a strong directional cast so it works rotated, luminous ochre core, subtle Greek Mediterranean feel. Match a polished illustrated game with a small charming astronaut girl. No text, no logos, no diagram, no geometric placeholder appearance. Output only the isolated terrain sprite.

Salida: `planet-terrain.png`.

Reading order: top left: elegant ancient Greek small temple with three columns, triangular pediment and dark open entrance; top middle: small round friendly retro spacecraft with copper trim, teal glass window, little landing feet, no fire; top right: old olive tree with lush sage canopy and twisted trunk. Every object independently isolated with clean true alpha on TRANSPARENT background, no scenery or floor joining neighboring cells, suitable for slicing cells into individual Phaser texture frames, not a moodboard, no UI. Crisp charming details visible at game scale.
